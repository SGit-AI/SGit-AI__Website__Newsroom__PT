**The research brief was telling ChatGPT an address that does not exist.** The contract every
outside assistant is asked to validate against was published at
`newsroom.sgit.ai/briefs/pt-newsroom-pack/08__research-briefs/research-schema.json`, and this site
is `pt.newsroom.sgit.ai` serving `briefs/pack/`. Two errors in one URL — wrong host, wrong
directory — in both research briefs and in the schema's own `$id`. The first delivery said so in
its own notes: *"O schema e o exemplo em newsroom.sgit.ai não estavam acessíveis nesta sessão; a
estrutura segue o resumo contratual do brief."* It then failed schema validation on twelve counts,
every one of them a closed list it could not read. **A contract nobody can fetch is not a
contract**, and the delivery that followed it is the evidence.

**So the closed lists are now in the brief itself, not only in the schema.** Section D spells out
`kind` (the delivery invented `instrumento_juridico` and `agenda_nacional`; there are eight kinds
and those are not among them), `confidence` (`média` carries its accent), entity `type` and the
`org:`/`politica:` id pattern (the delivery wrote `instrumento:rcm-2-2026`, and there is no such
prefix), and that `dates` is an array of objects rather than of strings. The schema is still the
gate. The brief now agrees with it in prose, so a tool that cannot fetch one still meets the other.

**The most useful correction came from counting which page lost the most claims.** Nine of the ten
unusable claims in delivery 1/8 rested on a single address. `diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/…`
renders its text by script: the fetcher froze 2,346 bytes of it carrying **22 characters of visible
text**, so no excerpt could be found in the bytes and every claim on it was dropped — including the
confirmation of the instrument behind the national AI agenda, which is the lead this newsroom most
wants. In the same delivery, `files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/…pdf` froze 228,725 bytes carrying
**30,808 characters**, and both claims on it were confirmed. The two are the same publisher and
often the same act. The brief now carries that as a rule: follow a `/dr/detalhe/` page to the
*Série I* PDF and cite the PDF. One line, and it is worth more than the rest of this release.

**The brief also now says what the newsroom already holds.** It listed the summit captures of 8 and
13 September and nothing since, so a second delivery would have re-fetched the eight policy pages
frozen on 14 September. They are now a table, marked with which two are unreadable and why, above a
plain statement of the single most wanted item: RCM n.º 2/2026 from a source the fetcher can read.

**No reader-facing page changes in this release.** The briefs are linked from `/entregas/` and
served as markdown — this site links, it does not reproduce — so correcting them corrects what the
next assistant is handed without republishing anything.
