**The front page stops being about itself.** Until this release the three blocks a reader met first
were the readability of the national register, an event that names its stages two ways, and a
speaker list with only one capture. Every one of them was a story about **this newsroom's own
plumbing**. They were honest, they were the only things the site could claim before it had
published anything, and they were the wrong thing to put in front of somebody who came to read
about AI in Portugal. They are gone, along with the empty container for the national AI agenda's
legal instrument — an article with no prose, describing work rather than saying anything.

**Four articles removed, five published in their place.** The front page now leads with the €25M
announced for AI in public administration, and carries the AI Act designation that is nowhere on
the government's own AI page and the Data Centres Plan read whole from the gazette's PDF. Which
article takes which slot is still a property of the article, declared in its own `artigo.json`;
what changed is the fallback — with no slot declared, published articles fill lead and the two
secondaries in order, so the front page can never be empty and can never link to an article that
no longer exists.

**The chips under the headline described a different article than the headline.** They were the
readability measurement, and they stayed put when the lead changed — a block of numbers about one
story sitting under the title of another. They are now the lead's own frozen sources, named by
**publisher and byte count** rather than by the id the register happens to carry: `src-pol-03`
means something to this repository and nothing to a reader.

**A closed issue is not an abandoned one.** Issue 001 — what instrument approves the national AI
agenda, and why can nobody read it — is closed and transferred, not dropped. The question now
lives in issue 006, the hunt for the Série I PDF of *Diário da República* n.º 5/2026, and in the
published article that says the agenda has 32 initiatives and its founding act still will not open.
Issue 002, commissioned by §11 of the brief and open without prose since the first day, is
published.

**What a reader now finds.** Five articles, all published, each carrying only claims re-found in
the frozen bytes; one story still in preparation and shown as such; and `/artigos/` as the complete
archive, grouped by the day each was published.
