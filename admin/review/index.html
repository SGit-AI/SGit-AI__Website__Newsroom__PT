The questions whose answer is the editor of record's, and what has changed since the others were answered. Nothing here decides anything: a decision is a file in the repository, written by a person, and this page only helps write it.

What this page does, and what it does not

Your answers stay in your browser and nowhere else. This site is static: it does not write to the repository, and a page that appeared to record a decision without recording it would be the one dishonest page on this publication. What it does is hold your words while you review — you can stop and come back — and hand them back as a block ready to paste into the agent that will turn them into files. Nothing is a decision until the file exists. A question with no verdict stays open, which is the safe state and what gate 13 already enforces. Silence is never a yes.
