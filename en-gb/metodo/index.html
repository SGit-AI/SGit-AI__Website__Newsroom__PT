Method · what this newsroom does, in this order

Obter · congelar · hashear · extrair · comparar

It is the path the section /portugal/ of newsroom.sgit.ai already runs, copied before anything was written here, because it is the only part of this method with proof that it works. Four of its properties hold up everything else.

1 · Captures are dated, not unique

One frozen copy proves a claim. A series proves a trajectory and shows what disappeared. In a subject that moves, the difference IS the news.

2 · The live site is never read when publishing

Extraction runs against the frozen copy, so a page cannot change underneath a claim without the change appearing as a new hash.

3 · The copies are .snapshot, not .html

They are bytes of other people's pages, kept as evidence. An extension that is not HTML stops them being served or indexed as pages of this site.

4 · The gate re-verifies every SHA-256 on every build

If a frozen copy has been edited, every claim resting on it loses its support and the build has to stop. Today: 92 files, all re-verified.

What this newsroom refuses, and where the refusal runs

The refusals run at the point of READING, not of presentation. A contact detail that reaches the data and is merely hidden by a template is one distracted cycle away from being published.

A recusaWhere it runs
No contact detail of a natural person build/extract.py, in the role sem_contactos, before the field reaches a file. Gate 5 checks the files afterwards.
Nenhuma biografia reproduzida The card's paragraph of prose is never read into a file. Gate 6 fails the build if any field is long enough to be a biography.

Nenhum alvo relatado como resultado O evento declara «150+ speakers»; isso é um plano. O que este site conta são os cartões da cópia congelada. O portão 4 falha a construção se um alvo aparecer como resultado.
No characterisation of a named person A tag says the page contains certain words. The connections between entities are made at organisation level.
The lexicon · a published formula

A published formula. Every entry is a regular expression that runs over the frozen text of a person's or an organisation's page; when it matches, a derived edge is born in the graph carrying the words it matched and nothing else.

A characterisation of nobody. A tag says that page contains those words. It does not say what the person is, does, knows or is worth. Not one sentence of the biography is copied anywhere: what stands is the word the expression caught, cut at 40 characters.

The labels are Portuguese because this site's reader is Portuguese. The PATTERNS are English because the source is English: the Startup Summit publishes its pages in English, and a Portuguese regular expression running over English prose would match nothing while appearing to work. Doing it the other way round would be exactly the error §6 of the brief describes — an ontology in one language behind an interface in another, wrong without looking broken. Ler o registo oficial

O Diário da República renderiza por script: obtido por HTTP, devolve poucos bytes e quase nenhum texto. Mas publica o diploma integral em PDF, e esse PDF chega cifrado com o manipulador de segurança padrão e com tipos subconjuntados — o que faz uma biblioteca genérica devolver zero caracteres, e devolvê-los em silêncio. Um zero devolvido por uma limitação do leitor é indistinguível, no ficheiro de resultados, de um zero devolvido por uma página vazia, e essa confusão faria esta redação publicar «não se consegue ler o registo nacional» quando a verdade seria «não implementámos o decifrador». build/pdf.py implements it, and reads the map /ToUnicode de cada tipo em vez de adivinhar o deslocamento dos glifos. Quando mesmo assim um PDF não abre, a razão fica registada ao lado da afirmação, e a afirmação é marcada «sem fonte legível» e nunca «excerto não encontrado».
