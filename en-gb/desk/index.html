The desk · the board, the mail and the runs

Nothing on this page is drawn by hand. The room is made of dados/redacao.json, which in turn is counted from the files in redacao/issues/, redacao/correio/, redacao/runs/ and of the work per agent at dados/comentarios.json. Click a bench to see what it does, what recusa to do, and what is waiting on it — and note that there is no way to move a card from here: a story's state lives in the files of its folder, and the «publicado» column is the editor of record's line. The mail comes from the files in redacao/correio/, and the runs from the files in redacao/runs/. A department writes only in its own folder, and gate 12 fails the build if a run's difference shows research writing prose or the desk touching a source. It is what makes this a newsroom and not a script.

The same board, without JavaScript

The room above needs a browser that runs modules. This version does not, and so it stays: half of this site's readers are machines, and a newsroom that could only be read by one of them would be the wrong irony. Both come out of the same files.
