Policy

Public policies announced and, where it can be found, the legal instrument that creates them.

What this section can claim today

What is in the PDFs of the Diário da República that this newsroom can read, and what it could not read and why.

What it cannot

That a policy does not exist because its instrument was not found. They are different things and the difference is the story.

An announcement and an instrument are two things. This site connects them when it can, and says when it cannot.
