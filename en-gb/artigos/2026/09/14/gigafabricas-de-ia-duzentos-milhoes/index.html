The Council of Ministers approved Portugal's entry into the European AI Gigafactories, with an investment of up to 200 million euros

The Conselho de Ministros communiqué of 25 June 2026 records the approval of a resolution allowing Portugal to join the European AI Gigafactories project. That is what the Government announced, with a date and an address. The instrument that enacts it was not located by this newsroom.

Published on 15 September 2026 by Dinis Cruz, the editor of record. Reading before publishing is the one thing an automated run of this site cannot do.

The same point in the communiqué says this is done through an investment of up to 200 million euros for acquiring computing capacity 2026-09-14/entregas/chatgpt-pt-newsroom-2026-09-14-politicas/src-pol-04.

Both sentences are in the communiqué, and the communiqué is frozen in this repository with its SHA-256. That is all this article claims.

What is missing, and why it is said rather than left out

Um comunicado do Conselho de Ministros anuncia que uma resolução foi aprovada. Não é a resolução. O ato publicado tem um número, uma data de publicação e um texto, e é o texto que diz o que fica efetivamente decidido — a que programa europeu adere Portugal, com que contrapartidas, através de que entidade, e o que significa «até».

This newsroom did not locate that instrument. It is not a claim that it does not exist: it is the record that, as of this edition, the announcement is found and the instrument is not.

This is the same distance that separates almost every policy story on this site. An announcement and an instrument are two things; this site connects them when it can, and says when it cannot.

What this article does not claim
  • It does not claim the investment was made. It claims it was announced as potentially reaching 200 million euros.
  • It does not claim no instrument exists. It claims this newsroom did not locate it, which is different.
  • It does not characterise the decision or who took it.

This list is here because the gap between what a source supports and what a reader may conclude is where a publication like this one does harm without saying anything false.
