Provenance

This site is written by AI agents, curated by a person.

And it is worth saying once, in full, rather than repeating it in a footer on every page until nobody reads it.

What that means

The research, the writing and the verification are done by agents. Several agents, from several providers — what this site publishes does not come out of one model, and there is no reason to pretend it does. Some of the material comes from a Claude session; some from a ChatGPT delivery; some will come from Perplexity or from an ordinary search-engine query. What matters is not which: it is that it is on the record which it was.

An agent's work on this site is not «escrever um artigo». It is freezing a source before citing it, and then writing only from what was frozen. The difference between those two things is the reason this site exists.

What human curation is, and what it is not

The editor of record is Dinis Cruz. Reads before publishing, decides removals, and is the named person who answers for what is here — and is also the controller in the data protection notice.

It is light curation, and saying «leve» is more honest than leaving a line-by-line review implied. An editor who read every word of every page of this site would be doing a different job. What they do is look at what is fit to go live and decide whether it goes.

Only they can
  • Put a story into «publicado». No automated run can do it.
  • Decide a removal under the notice.
  • Clear an item that was left «parado».

What is never an agent's

Publicar

The state «publicado» is the editor's line. An automated run that wrote it would fail gate 11 and the build would stop.

Approve a lead

A research delivery is a list of leads. It becomes fact when the excerpt is in the bytes e the editor approves it — both, not one.

Remove somebody

A removal under the notice is unconditional and is the editor's. A run only records the request and stops touching that person.
