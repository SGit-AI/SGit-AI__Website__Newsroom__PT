Institutions

Public bodies, regulators, research units — and what can or cannot be read from the record they publish.

What this section can claim today

That three pages of the national register were fetched on a date, and how many visible characters each returned to an automated reader.

What it cannot

That an institution is hiding something. A page that renders by script is a common technical choice.

The legibility of the public register is measurable, datable and hashable. The intention of whoever publishes it is not.

Editor (8)

The house that publishes a frozen page: a public body, a newspaper, a portal, an event's own site. It comes out of the register's publisher field by the `editor_de_fonte` formula, and it says who PUBLISHES the page — not who wrote it, not who operates it. It exists as a type of its own because the definition of Institution is «um organismo público, um regulador ou uma unidade de investigação», and a newspaper is none of those: widening that definition to fit here would change what it promises.
