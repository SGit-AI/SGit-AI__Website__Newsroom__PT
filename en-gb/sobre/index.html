About · what is real and what is not

A map of an ecosystem, built backwards: the evidence first, the claim second.

What is real today
  • 92 frozen files from 1 capture(s), each with its SHA-256, all re-verified on every build.
  • A graph of 312 nodes and 942 edges, in which every node names the frozen source it came from.
  • An ontology of Portuguese verbs, each with a distinct inverse, in which a path reads aloud.
  • A data protection notice published before the first page that names anybody.
  • A flow of research deliveries in which every excerpt is searched for in the bytes before it counts as a claim.
What is not yet
  • No story published. The first three are open as issues at the desk, in the state «procurado». Publishing is the editor of record's line, and no automated run can write it.
  • The company layer cannot be complete. There is no open commercial register in Portugal: the register is per company, paid, and behind an access code, and the beneficial ownership register stopped being publicly accessible in 2022. The honest claim is a different one and it is better — these are the organisations that appear in European and national public funding, with amounts and dates.
  • Three of the eight sections are empty and say so on the page itself, rather than being hidden from the navigation.
  • No agent level beyond A has been tested. The brief requires testing level B first and treating the result as a finding either way.
The language

This site is natively Portuguese and is not a translation of anything. The graph's verbs are Portuguese and the English is an annotation — not the other way round, which is the likeliest way for a site like this to be quietly wrong, because nothing on it looks broken. Session titles and the names of organisations and people stay verbatim, in the language the source wrote them in: a transcription is a claim. And the accents are data. The corpus this method comes from mandates pure ASCII; here the gate runs the other way and asserts that the accents are present and came from the source, and not from a list somebody typed.
