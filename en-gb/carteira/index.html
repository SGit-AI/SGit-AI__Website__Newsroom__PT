The wallet

Every page on this site costs one cent to open, and the record of how much you have spent is here.

The parent site argues, across several essays, that whoever creates the fact should be paid for it. This is that argument made concrete in a publication that actually exists: every page has a price, opening it debits the wallet, the wallet tops up when it empties, and the record of what was spent is legible — it is below, and it is yours, nobody else's.

Where the money would go

In a real version, the cent would go to whoever produced the frozen source the page rests on — and that is why this publication always records which registo is, seen from this angle, the list of creditors.

The whole argument lives at newsroom.sgit.ai, and is not repeated here: content exists once.

Written by AI agents, curated by a named human editor. Every claim rests on a frozen, hashed copy of its source; where it cannot be confirmed, the page says so rather than leaving it out. Who did what.
