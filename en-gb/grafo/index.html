The types
TipoDefinition Nodes
EventoA dated and located gathering that publishes a programme and a list of who speaks.1
SessãoAn item from an event's published programme, with a day, a time and usually a stage. Title verbatim, in the language the event published it in.15
PalcoA named room or stage where sessions are scheduled. This event names its stages two different ways on different pages of its own site.2
PessoaA person named in the speaker list the event published, in their professional capacity. Name, listed role, listed organisation, edges, the topics the event itself assigns them, and the words on their page that match a published lexicon. Never a biography, never a contact detail.70
OrganisationAn organisation a person is LISTED under on their speaker card. Derived from the cards, never written by hand; markers such as «Independent» are flagged, not removed.67
LocalA place where something happens. It is not a person, so its address may be held.1
InstituiçãoA public body, a regulator or a research unit. Distinct from Organisation because the source and the obligations differ: an institution publishes a record, a company publishes communications.3
PolíticaA public policy announced and — when it exists and when it can be found — the legal instrument that creates it. The second is frequently the problem.0
CapturaA dated capture of the sources: every page fetched, frozen as bytes in this repository, and hashed.1
SourceA frozen page from a capture, with its SHA-256. It is the thing a claim walks back to.92
CoberturaThe page a third party published on this subject, fetched, frozen and hashed like any other source. Linked, never reproduced.0
HistóriaA piece this publication wrote, resting on named frozen sources and read by the editor of record before publishing.6
TemaA topic the event lists on a person's page, verbatim. Read from the frozen page, never derived: their vocabulary, not ours.23
SetorA sector named by the published lexicon and matched on somebody's page. The edge carries the matched words. It says the page contains those words; nothing more.7
TecnologiaA technology named by the published lexicon and matched on a frozen page. Derived by formula; the edge shows the evidence.8
IdeiaA recurring idea from founders' conversation, named by the published lexicon. Derived by formula.4
ServiçoA kind of service a frozen page says somebody provides, matched by the published lexicon. Categories, not product names.3
ProdutoA kind of product a frozen page names, matched by the published lexicon. Categories, not product names.1
EditorThe house that publishes a frozen page: a public body, a newspaper, a portal, an event's own site. It comes out of the register's publisher field by the `editor_de_fonte` formula, and it says who PUBLISHES the page — not who wrote it, not who operates it. It exists as a type of its own because the definition of Institution is «um organismo público, um regulador ou uma unidade de investigação», and a newspaper is none of those: widening that definition to fit here would change what it promises.8
